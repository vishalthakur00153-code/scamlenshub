Common Scam Archetypes & How They Work

Scammers rely on predictable psychological triggers. Knowing these patterns helps you stay protected.

🎓

The Internship Training Fee Trap

You receive an offer without a rigorous interview, but are asked to pay a "refundable registration fee", "laptop delivery charge", or "training deposit".

Golden Rule: Legitimate employers never charge candidates money to begin working.
⚡

Artificial Urgency & Panic

"Your bank account will be closed in 2 hours" or "Your electricity will be disconnected tonight". Attackers create false emergencies so you react before thinking.

Golden Rule: High urgency is a psychological lever. Disconnect and call the verified helpline.
🔑

The Credential & OTP Heist

Impersonating technical support or courier customs agents, callers or texts insist on you sharing a 6-digit OTP or password to "cancel a charge" or "verify identity".

Golden Rule: An OTP is a digital signature. Anyone asking for it is trying to access your funds.
🌐

Camouflaged Links & IP Addresses

Links that use numeric IP addresses (http://192.168...) or misleading subdomains (paypal.security-update.com) to mimic trusted banking or shopping websites.

Golden Rule: Read the root domain immediately preceding the top-level extension (.com).
